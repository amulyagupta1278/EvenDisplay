MacBook Display Troubleshooting

Why your MacBook Pro has dim vertical bands — and what actually helps

Seeing sharp top-to-bottom dim columns or patches on gray and white screens? Before buying a repair or running questionable "pixel fixer" videos, run these four quick checks to pinpoint the exact cause.

Step 1: Does macOS have a built-in pixel cleaner?

No. macOS does not include a manual Pixel Cleaning or Pixel Refresh button in System Settings. Touch Bar and Liquid Retina MacBook Pros use IPS LCD or Mini-LED panels (which do not use OLED cleaning cycles), and OLED Mac displays manage voltage compensation automatically during sleep.

Step 2: Isolate hardware vs. software in 30 seconds

Open a full-screen 50% Gray test screen (using EvenDisplay Studio) and press Cmd + Shift + 3 to take a screenshot. View that image on your phone or an external monitor.

If the dim vertical bands do not appear in the screenshot — and still appear on the Apple boot screen — your GPU is healthy and the defect is inside the built-in display panel (typically an LCD column driver / COF bond or a worn hinge display flex cable).

Step 3: Run the lid-angle check & 15-minute IPS sweep

Slowly tilt your MacBook lid between 45° and 130° while viewing 50% Gray or 10% Dark Gray. If the bands flicker or change darkness with angle, the hinge flex cable is wearing out.

If the dim patch matches an app layout you leave open for hours, run a 15-minute White & Gray Sweep in EvenDisplay to clear temporary IPS image retention.

Step 4: Visually blend permanent dim columns (100% reversible)

If the columns are stable hardware brightness offsets and a full display replacement isn't worth the cost right now, EvenDisplay lets you drag two movable compensation bands directly over your dim strips, verify them across Gray, White, and RGB, and float a click-through overlay exclusively on your built-in MacBook display.

Nothing touches macOS system files, ICC profiles, or NVRAM — pressing Cmd + Option + 0 or quitting the app restores your stock display immediately.
