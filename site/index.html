FAQ

Straight answers for MacBook owners.

Everything you need to know before testing your display or installing the menu-bar utility.

Does MacBook Pro have a built-in pixel cleaner or screen test?

No. macOS does not include a manual Pixel Cleaning, Pixel Refresh, or full-screen uniformity test in System Settings. EvenDisplay gives MacBook users a free 9-color uniformity test, IPS pixel conditioning sweep, and reversible screen band compensator.

What MacBook screen issues can EvenDisplay help with?

EvenDisplay visually balances dim vertical bands and uneven color casts using a reversible click-through overlay, clears temporary IPS image retention and ghosting with full-screen sweeps, spots stuck or dead pixels, and provides a 2-minute lid-angle hardware diagnostic for MacBook Pro and Air screens.

Will EvenDisplay tint my external monitor?

No. The native macOS app queries CGDisplayIsBuiltin and anchors the overlay exclusively to your built-in MacBook panel.

How do I undo everything cleanly?

Press Cmd+Option+0 for an instant stock screen, double-click any slider to reset it to default, click Factory Reset in Preferences, or quit/delete EvenDisplay.app. Zero system files are ever modified.
