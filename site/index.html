What software can solve — and what only hardware can.

No misleading miracle claims. Read the MacBook vertical bands diagnostic guide to tell hardware wear from temporary image retention in two minutes.

What EvenDisplay solves

  • Visual seam blending: Lifts dim vertical columns on grays and subtly dims surrounding bright areas on white pages so seams stop distracting your eyes.
  • Temporary IPS image retention: Includes a full-screen White & Gray Sweep, RGB subpixel cycle, and pixel snow conditioner with automatic wake lock.
  • 100% reversible user-space design: Never modifies macOS system files, ColorSync ICC profiles, GPU registers, or NVRAM.
  • Instant safety controls: Full Undo/Redo (Cmd+Z), Compare Original (C), named Light/Dark checkpoints, and instant Revert to Stock (Cmd+Option+0).

Hardware realities

  • Column-driver (COF) & flex cable wear: Sharp top-to-bottom dim stripes that survive a reboot are physical hardware defects inside the laminated display assembly.
  • Lid-angle check built in: Tilt your MacBook lid on 75% Gray or 10% Dark Gray. If band intensity shifts with hinge angle, the hinge display flex cable is wearing out.
  • Practical daily workaround: Use EvenDisplay while deciding between an AppleCare+ repair, third-party screen swap, or external monitor setup.

Common questions from MacBook owners

Straight answers to the exact questions people hit when dim vertical bands appear on a MacBook Pro or Air.

Does MacBook Pro have a built-in pixel cleaner?

No. macOS has no manual Pixel Cleaning or Pixel Refresh toggle. IPS LCD and Mini-LED MacBook Pros do not use OLED cleaning cycles, while OLED panels compensate automatically during sleep.

Can software physically repair vertical dark bands?

No software can repair cracked COF traces or worn hinge flex cables. EvenDisplay visually compensates the luminance mismatch with a reversible click-through overlay so the display stays comfortable to work on.

Will EvenDisplay tint my external monitor?

No. The native macOS app queries CGDisplayIsBuiltin and anchors the overlay exclusively to your built-in MacBook panel.

How do I undo everything cleanly?

Press Cmd+Option+0 for an instant stock screen, click Factory Reset in Preferences, or quit/delete EvenDisplay.app. Zero system files are ever modified.

First-launch note for free unsigned builds

To keep EvenDisplay 100% free and MIT open source without an annual Apple Developer fee, release builds are ad-hoc signed rather than Apple-notarized. On first launch:

  • Open EvenDisplay.app once, then go to System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.
  • Or clear the browser quarantine flag in Terminal:
xattr -cr /Applications/EvenDisplay.app
