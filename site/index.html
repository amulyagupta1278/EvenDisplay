01

Hover-only band edges for unobstructed matching

Band borders stay completely invisible while you move sliders. Hover within 20px of a band edge to reveal its hairline guide, drag it over your dim strip, and move back to the controls — the line vanishes immediately so your eyes judge only the seam.

02

Brightness, Temp, Tint & per-channel RGB trim

Worn LCD column drivers often introduce subtle green, magenta, or warm casts alongside dimming. Dial Brightness (±50%), Temp (±40%), Green/Magenta Tint (±40%), Fine RGB, or Outside Dim on pure white — double-click any slider to reset to default.

03

Verify locked across 9 colors, then sync to macOS

Flip across 50% Gray, 25% Gray, 75% Gray, 10% Dark, White, Red, Green, Blue, and Black while your bands remain locked. Export .evendisplay.json into EvenDisplay.app for a click-through overlay anchored strictly to your built-in MacBook panel.

FAQ

Straight answers for MacBook owners.

Everything you need to know before testing your screen or installing the menu-bar utility.

Does MacBook Pro have a built-in pixel cleaner?

No. macOS does not include a manual Pixel Cleaning or Pixel Refresh button in System Settings. IPS LCD and Mini-LED MacBook Pros do not use OLED cleaning cycles, and OLED panels run background voltage compensation automatically during sleep.

Can software physically repair vertical dark bands?

No software can repair cracked COF traces or worn hinge flex cables. EvenDisplay visually compensates the luminance and color-cast mismatch with a reversible click-through overlay so the display stays comfortable to work on.

Will EvenDisplay tint my external monitor?

No. The native macOS app queries CGDisplayIsBuiltin and anchors the overlay exclusively to your built-in MacBook panel.

How do I undo everything cleanly?

Press Cmd+Option+0 for an instant stock screen, double-click any slider to reset it to default, click Factory Reset in Preferences, or quit/delete EvenDisplay.app. Zero system files are ever modified.
